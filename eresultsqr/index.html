WAEC QR Validator

Privacy Policy

What happens when you scan a result slip — in plain terms.

Effective 11 August 2026 Applies to Android app, com.eresultsqrscanner.app
In short

WAEC QR Validator does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The camera is used only to read a QR code on your screen, and the whole verification happens on your device — nothing is ever sent anywhere.

01 What the camera sees

The app asks for camera access for one reason: to point it at the QR code printed on a WAEC result slip and read the code. The camera feed is processed live, in memory, to detect the code — it is never recorded, saved as a photo or video, or sent off your device.

If you decline camera access, the app can't scan a code, but nothing else about it is affected.

02 What happens to the code

Once a QR code is detected, its contents are decrypted using a key built into the app — entirely on your device, with no server involved. The decrypted result details are shown on screen for you to read.

If you tap Copy, the decoded text is placed on your device's clipboard. That's the only action that moves this data anywhere, and it only happens when you choose to do it.

03 What we don't do

  • No account, sign-in, or personal profile of any kind.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No network requests at all — the app works fully offline.
  • No storage of scanned results after you close the app.

04 Permissions at a glance

Permission Used for Leaves your device?
Camera Reading the QR code on a result slip, live Never

05 Children's privacy

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age — there's simply no data collection to speak of.
